OBJECTIVE
Close the live Telegram verification gap for the currently supported native
transport. Prove that Serenox can authorize an account through the existing
login flow, start the daemon non-interactively from a persisted session, verify
the saved account identity, ingest bounded live updates, execute supported text
and media actions, recover from disconnects, and shut down without leaked
resources. Keep unsupported Telegram surfaces explicit instead of claiming full
Hikka, Pyrogram, or Aiogram compatibility.

The result must distinguish three states clearly:

1. verified by deterministic unit, adapter, recorded, or supervised tests;
2. verified by an opt-in live-account smoke test;
3. explicitly unsupported or not live-verified.
